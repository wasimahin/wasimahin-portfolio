Privacy
Notice

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Wasi Mahin. The short version: no cookies, no accounts, no ads, and nothing that follows you around the web. Two things do collect data — cookieless visitor analytics, and the contact form if you choose to use it. Both are described below, in full.

Browser storage

The site sets one functional sessionStorage flag so the intro animation plays only once per visit. It contains no personal data, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted automatically when you close the tab.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may log visitor IP addresses and basic request data for security and operational purposes. That logging is governed by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy; I never see or store that data.

Fonts & assets

Every font, image and script that makes up this page is self-hosted. There is no font CDN, no social widget, no advertising network and no tag manager. The only request that leaves this origin is the analytics beacon described above, and it is served by the same company that already hosts the site.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is switched on deliberately, so I can see whether the work here reaches anyone.

It is cookieless. It does not fingerprint your device, does not build a profile of you, and cannot follow you to other websites. What it records is aggregate: page views, referring site, country, browser and device type. I can see that a page was viewed from a country. I cannot see who you are.

The data is processed by Cloudflare under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Blocking it changes nothing — a content blocker will stop the beacon loading and the page behaves identically.

Contact form

If you send a message, the form collects exactly what you type: your name, your email address and your message. Nothing else, and no hidden fields.

Your browser sends it to this site's own domain, not to a third-party form service. From there it is relayed to my inbox by Resend, an email delivery provider, along with the country your request came from. I use it only to reply. It is never added to a mailing list, never sold, and never shared.

Prefer not to use the form? Email me directly — it reaches exactly the same place.

Security

Cloudflare runs automated bot and abuse detection in front of this site. That is standard for any site on their network and is how it stays online. It is a security measure, not analytics, and it is not used to build a profile of you.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and email open services with their own privacy policies. Once you leave this site, their rules apply, not mine.

Email

If you email me at wasimahin@gmail.com, I use your message and address only to reply. Nothing is added to mailing lists or shared with anyone.

Questions

Anything unclear, or want something corrected? Email me — I reply fast.
