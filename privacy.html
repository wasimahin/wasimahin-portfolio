Privacy
Notice

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Wasi Mahin. The short version: this site collects nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no contact forms, no accounts.

Browser storage

The site sets one functional sessionStorage flag so the intro animation plays only once per visit. It contains no personal data, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted automatically when you close the tab.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and basic request data for security and operational purposes. That logging is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement; I never see or store that data.

Fonts & assets

All fonts, scripts, and images are self-hosted. Loading this site makes no requests to third-party servers — your IP address is not shared with any font CDN, analytics service, or social widget.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and email open services with their own privacy policies. Once you leave this site, their rules apply, not mine.

Email

If you email me at wasimahin@gmail.com, I use your message and address only to reply. Nothing is added to mailing lists or shared with anyone.

Questions

Anything unclear, or want something corrected? Email me — I reply fast.
