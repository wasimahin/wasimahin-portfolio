Tahúr: a card game, designed and directed

Tahúr is a free blackjack roguelike you play in the browser, head to head against a rival, with powerups that bend the rules and online play for up to 128 players. I designed it, playtested it and made every product call. Claude, Anthropic's AI, wrote the code and the dialogue, composed the music as code and drew the art as code. No image generators and no AI voices.

Who did what

Me

  • The core rules: one hidden and one visible card each, win 5 sets or 3 in a row, at most 3 powerups.
  • Progression, the story's five-chapter shape, the name, the brand and the visual direction.
  • The business model and the product rules below.
  • Every feature and release call, and a playtest of every build.

Claude

  • All the code: engine, interface, servers, about 34,000 lines of TypeScript and 349 automated tests.
  • The dialogue, within the story structure I set.
  • The art and the music, both made in code.
  • Running tests and deploying, each production deploy on my go-ahead.

Playtesting drove it

  • Testers said early builds felt like pure luck. I redesigned the rules around raising, calling and folding and final stands. A skilled strategy then beat a fixed “stand on 17” strategy 61% of the time in AI-vs-AI simulations, up from 51%.
  • Testers couldn't read the emoji icons, so the game got real art.
  • When one build looked AI-made to me, I had the whole game redesigned in a pulp-comic style with its own design rules.

Product rules

  • No real money, ever. Coins are won at the table only and can't be bought or cashed out.
  • Honest mystery boxes. Earned coins only, the odds printed on every box, and a legendary guaranteed within 30.
  • Nothing for sale in the browser, and no ads.
  • Everyone starts even online. Multiplayer duels open with a draft, so skill decides them.
  • Privacy by default. No tracking cookies; anonymous play counts that players can switch off.
  • Accessible. Large text, reduced flashing, a plain-font option, big tap targets, keyboard and controller play.

Launch

Public in Early Access at tahurgame.com on 12 October 2026, after a launch pass on security, privacy and accounts that I set the requirements for. Within five days Google had indexed the site and the IGDB game database had listed it.
