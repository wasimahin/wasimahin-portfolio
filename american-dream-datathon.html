Is the American Dream still attainable?

For the AIS Annual Datathon 2026 at CSULB, my team asked whether income growth in the United States still buys a better life, once you account for where people live. Our Tableau analysis placed 3rd of 10 teams.

3rdof 10 teams
RPPRegional Price Parities, to compare purchasing power
Tableaufor the analysis and the visualizations

The question

Headline income can rise while what it buys stays flat, and the same salary goes further in some states than others. We set out to measure income in real terms and see whether states were catching up with each other.

Method

We adjusted U.S. income data for the cost of living with Regional Price Parities (RPP), the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis's measure of how price levels differ between states. That turns nominal income into purchasing power you can compare across states.

What we found

  • Purchasing power differs widely between regions once prices are taken into account.
  • States showed limited convergence: the gaps between them were narrowing only slowly.

My part

I analyzed the income data with the cost-of-living adjustment and built data visualizations to communicate the findings clearly.
