03. projects

PROJ 01

Snehin Check Request Helper

The Problem

Manual PDF-to-ERP data entry consumed significant staff hours per batch. No API existed. No ERP credentials available. The only path was a human typing into Business Central, field by field.

The Approach

Zero-API desktop RPA: parses vendor invoice PDFs with pdfplumber, extracts structured data, then drives the Business Central UI directly via pyautogui — no credentials, no integration, just results.
